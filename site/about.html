About Austin Excel Pest & Lawn Control

Locally owned, Central Texas focused, and doing this work since 1998.

Our story

A local company that started in South Austin.

Austin Excel Pest & Lawn Control opened in 1998 in South Austin. Since then we have grown into a full pest, rodent, wildlife and lawn-pest operation serving Austin, Buda, Kyle and the surrounding Hill Country from our base at 175 Warehouse Drive in Buda.

We are not a national franchise reading from a script. We treat for the pests that are actually a problem here in Central Texas — and we build a plan around your specific property rather than a one-size-fits-all package.

Many of our customers also work with our sister company, Research Turf Management, for landscaping and hardscape projects.
