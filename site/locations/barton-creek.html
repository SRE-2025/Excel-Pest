Pest Control in Barton Creek, Texas

Travis County · Family-owned since 1998

Barton Creek is greenbelt country — upscale homes tucked against limestone bluffs, live oaks and canyon land on the western edge of Austin. That beautiful setting is also prime scorpion and wildlife habitat.

Homes backing to the greenbelt see scorpions, spiders and the occasional wildlife visitor. We treat the perimeter and seal entry points so the wild stays outside.
