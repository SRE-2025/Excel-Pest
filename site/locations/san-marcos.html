Pest Control in San Marcos, Texas

Hays County · Family-owned since 1998

San Marcos pairs a spring-fed river and a major university with heat and humidity — a combination that keeps mosquitoes, roaches and rodents busy. Rental turnover near Texas State and riverside humidity make consistent pest control essential here.

From family homes on the hills to rentals near the square and the river, we handle the roaches and mosquitoes the humidity feeds, the rodents that follow, and the scorpions that come with the surrounding limestone country.
